# Exhibit A

The exhibit is part of the verified statement. It must let the owner tell
whether the work was done and whether the charges are fair, and its balance
must equal the amount claimed, to the cent. No internal notes, ratings or
document sources: those stay in the work file.

## When there are invoices

**Reconciliation**

| Invoice / item | Date | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Invoice [no.] | [date] | $[ ] |
| Approved change order [no.] | [date] | $[ ] |
| Payment received | [date] | ($[ ]) |
| Credit / back-charge | [date] | ($[ ]) |
| **Balance claimed** | | **$[amount claimed]** |

Then the invoices themselves, in the order listed.

**Reconciliation**

| | Amount |
|---|---|
| Total labor and materials (above) | $[ ] |
| Payments received ([dates]) | ($[ ]) |
| Credits | ($[ ]) |
| **Balance claimed** | **$[amount claimed]** |

Write descriptions the owner can recognize: "Tear-off and replacement of
north and east roof slopes, 22 squares, architectural shingles" rather than
"roofing labor". Separate labor and materials where the client's records allow
it. Where the contract was a fixed price, a line for the contract price, a line
per approved change order, and the payments is enough, with the contract
attached.
